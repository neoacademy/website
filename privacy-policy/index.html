Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026
The short version: Neo Games does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app runs entirely on your device, works fully offline, and has no accounts, ads, or analytics.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Neo Games mobile app (“the app”, “we”, “us”), published by Neo Academy, handles information when you or your child use it. Neo Games is an educational game designed for young children, and we have built it to need no personal data at all.

1. Information we collect

None. Neo Games does not ask for, collect, or transmit any personal information. Specifically, the app does not:

  • Require an account, sign-up, name, email, or phone number
  • Collect location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers
  • Include advertising, ad networks, or third-party analytics
  • Send any data off your device or require an internet connection to play

2. How the app works

All game content — images, sounds, and progress within a session — is stored and processed locally on your device. Because nothing is transmitted to us or to any third party, there is no personal data for us to see, store, or share.

3. Children’s privacy

Neo Games is intended for children and families. We take children’s privacy seriously and have designed the app to comply with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation provisions for children (GDPR-K), and Google Play’s Families policy. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

4. Device permissions

Neo Games only uses the device capabilities needed to play audio and display its built-in learning content. Any storage the app uses stays on your device and is never uploaded. The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, or contacts.

5. Third-party services & data sharing

Neo Games does not integrate advertising SDKs, analytics services, or social logins, and it does not sell or share any information with third parties — because it does not collect any in the first place.

6. Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices ever change, we will update this policy and revise the “Last updated” date above before the change takes effect. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
